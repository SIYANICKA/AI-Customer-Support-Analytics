# 5 Business Insights

1. **Billing & Payments is the largest complaint category**, with 185 of 1,000 complaints (18.5%).
2. **Payment failed but money was deducted is the most frequently reported individual issue**, appearing 52 times.
3. **Returns & Refunds has the longest average resolution time**, at 42.2 hours, making it the strongest candidate for workflow/SLA improvement.
4. **Returns & Refunds has the lowest average CSAT**, at 4.2/5, indicating a customer-experience risk area.
5. **28.8% of complaints are high priority.** This supports automated triage so urgent complaints reach the correct support queue faster.
